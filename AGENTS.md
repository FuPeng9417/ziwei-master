# Development instructions

- Treat all chart calculations as unverified until their source, convention profile, independent reference cases, and tests are documented.
- Never fabricate placements or present a placeholder as a chart result.
- Keep San He, Fei Xing, Qin Tian Si Hua, and He Luo rules in separate modules.
- Preserve both civil-time and true-solar-time inputs/outputs and record the method used.
- Record provenance and licensing for every external rule, dataset, or quoted text.
- `Renhuai123/ziwei-doushu` is a possible MIT-code reference only pending revision-specific audit; its datasets have separate terms.
- `ziweiknows/ziwei-chart` is GPL-3.0 and reference-only; do not copy its code.
- Prefer synthetic test data. Do not add personal birth data without permission and a redistribution basis.
- Explain uncertainty and competing conventions in user-facing output.
